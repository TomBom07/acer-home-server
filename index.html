Acer Home Server Privacy policy
PRIVATE • PERSONAL USE

Backups, under my control.

Acer Home Server is a personal utility that uses Rclone to read my selected Google Drive GoodNotes backup folder and copy its contents to a self-hosted Nextcloud server.

How it works

Google Drive→ Rclone on Acer→ Nextcloud

Read-only Google Drive permission. No public account creation. Files are copied privately to the owner's Nextcloud installation, not served from this website.
